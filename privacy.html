Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Mama Said (www.mamasaidapp.com) is a product of DowMeno LLC, an Oklahoma company ("we," "us"). This policy explains, in plain words, what information we collect, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The cards you make are saved on your own phone or computer, not on our servers.
  • When you talk with Mama or tap Hear Mama, your words go to ElevenLabs, the company that powers Mama's voice and conversation.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your full card number.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.
  • Questions or requests: hello@mamasaidapp.com

1. The cards you make

The words, pictures, music and voice recordings you add to a card, and the cards you keep in My Cards, are stored in your web browser on your own device. We do not receive or keep them unless you choose to send a card to someone or email it to us.

Each browser keeps its own copy, so cards saved in Safari won't appear in another app or on another device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

When you send or save a card, it goes wherever you choose (for example, Messages, Mail or your Photos). Those apps follow their own privacy rules.

2. Talking with Mama

When you tap Mama's face or Talk with Mama, your browser asks permission to use your microphone. If you allow it:

  • Your voice and what you say are sent to ElevenLabs so Mama can understand you and answer out loud. ElevenLabs uses AI language-model providers to help write Mama's replies.
  • ElevenLabs may keep a recording and written transcript of the conversation. We can view these to improve Mama and to fix problems.
  • Each visit with Mama lasts up to 3 minutes. The microphone stops when the visit ends or when you tap to say goodbye.

Please don't share private details you wouldn't want recorded, such as account numbers or medical information. Mama is an AI. She offers encouragement, not medical, legal or financial advice.

3. "Hear Mama" and video cards

When you tap Hear Mama, the title and message of your card are sent through our website to ElevenLabs to create Mama's voice reading it. The audio comes back to your device.

Video cards are put together on your own device. The video is not uploaded to us.

4. "Speak My Message"

This button uses your phone's or computer's own speech-to-text (for example, Apple's or Google's). Your speech is handled by that company under its privacy policy, not by Mama Said, and it does not use ElevenLabs.

5. Payments and memberships

Free trials, memberships and gift codes are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details and email address. We receive your name, email, plan and payment status. We do not see or store your full card number.

6. When you email us

If you write to hello@mamasaidapp.com, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them to answer you. Our email is handled through Google (Gmail).

7. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider, Microsoft Azure, keeps basic technical records, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to run and protect the site. We load fonts from Google Fonts and a voice tool from the jsDelivr network, and those services may also see your IP address. We do not use advertising trackers.

8. Who helps us

We share information only with the companies that help run Mama Said, and only for that purpose:

  • ElevenLabs: Mama's voice and conversations
  • Stripe: payments and memberships
  • Microsoft Azure: website hosting
  • Google: email and website fonts

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our users or the service. If Mama Said is ever sold or merged, this policy would continue to protect the information we hold.

9. How long we keep information

We keep conversation records and account information only as long as we need them to provide and improve Mama Said, run your membership, or follow the law. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

10. Your choices

  • Microphone: you can say no, or turn it off anytime in your phone or browser settings. You can still make cards without it.
  • Your cards: tap Start Over, delete cards from My Cards, or clear your browser data.
  • Your information: email us to ask what we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • Your membership: you can cancel anytime. Email us and we'll help.

11. Children

Mama Said is made for adults. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used Mama Said, email us and we will delete their information.

12. Keeping information safe

The site uses secure (HTTPS) connections, and we work with established companies to protect your information. No website can promise perfect security, but we take care with what you share.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Big changes will be explained on the website.
