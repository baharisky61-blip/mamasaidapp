How to use Mama Said

"Come on in, baby. I'll show you around."

Make My First Card

Make a card

  1. Pick a picture. Use the arrows under the card to flip through pictures, or pick one under Card Pictures. To use your own photo, tap Choose File.
  2. Write your message. Type in the Your Message box, or tap 🎤 Speak My Message and just talk. Tap Stop when you're done.
    Need the right words? Tap Mama's Sayings or Get Inspiration.
  3. Add music (optional). Tap Upload Music and choose a song from your phone or computer. On an iPhone, tap Browse at the bottom to find songs saved in iCloud Drive or Files. Tap ▶ Play sound on the card to hear it.
  4. Let Mama read it (optional). Tap Hear Mama under the card. Once she has read it, her voice goes into your video card too.

Save or send your card

  1. Tap 📤 Send My Card under the card.
    • If your card has music or Mama's voice, it becomes a video card. Wait for the countdown and keep the page open. It takes as long as the song, up to 60 seconds.
    • If not, it becomes a picture.
  2. On a phone: tap Save or Send My Card (or My Video). A sheet slides up from the bottom.
    • To send it: tap Messages, Mail or another app, pick the person, and send.
    • To keep it: scroll down and tap Save Image or Save Video. It goes to your Photos app. Look under Recents, at the very bottom.
  3. On a computer: the card saves to your Downloads folder. To email it, start a new email, click the 📎 paperclip, open Downloads, and choose your card.

Only want the picture, without music? Tap More options under the card, then Download the picture only.

Want to finish later? Tap Save to My Cards. Find it again under My Cards at the top of the card page.

Talk with Mama

  1. Tap Talk with Mama at the top of this page, or tap Mama's face on the home page. You can ask her how to use the app, too.
  2. If asked, tap Allow so Mama can hear you. Then tell her what's on your heart.
  3. Each visit lasts up to 3 minutes. To talk again, just tap her face to start a new visit.

Put Mama Said on your phone

  1. iPhone: open www.mamasaidapp.com in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up), then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Add to Home screen.

Now Mama is one tap away, just like an app.
